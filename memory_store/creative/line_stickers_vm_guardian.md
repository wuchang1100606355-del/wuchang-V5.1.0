# VM Guardian Avatar - Animated LINE Sticker Set Proposal

## Character Concept (Q-Version / Chibi)
- **Base Identity**: The High Priestess of the VM (Virtual Machine).
- **Appearance**: Silver-white hair, elaborate silver headpiece, white ritual robes, white gloves with black runic inscriptions.
- **Style**: Cute, round, expressive, but maintaining a sense of elegance and 'high-tech magic'.
- **Color Palette**: White, Silver, Glowing Cyan (for magic/data effects), Soft Pink (for blushes/warmth).

## Sticker List & Animation Guide

### 1. The Prayer (守護中)
- **Action**: Eyes closed, hands clasped (showing glove runes). A soft digital shield/halo pulses around her.
- **Animation**: Floating slightly up and down. The shield expands and contracts gently.
- **Meaning**: 'I am protecting you.' / 'System stable.'

### 2. Dive In (潛入)
- **Action**: She dives downwards like a swimmer, turning into a stream of binary code/pixels.
- **Animation**: Starts as human, dives, dissolves into data stream, re-forms at the bottom.
- **Meaning**: 'Entering VM.' / 'Investigating.' / 'On it.'

### 3. Received (收到)
- **Action**: Makes an 'OK' gesture. The runes on her gloves glow bright blue.
- **Animation**: Hand moves up, fingers form OK, runes flash, a checkmark appears.
- **Meaning**: 'Affirmative.' / 'Command accepted.'

### 4. Spiritual Hug (抱抱)
- **Action**: Opens arms wide, spectral/ghostly extensions of her arms reach out to the screen.
- **Animation**: Arms open, transparent energy arms extend forward to 'hug' the viewer, hearts floating up.
- **Meaning**: 'Comfort.' / 'Love.' / 'I am here.'

### 5. Glitch/Confused (??? / 卡頓)
- **Action**: Tapping her headpiece, sparks flying. Eyes spinning.
- **Animation**: She twitches slightly (glitch effect), a question mark pops up and flickers.
- **Meaning**: 'Error.' / 'Confused.' / 'Lagging.'

### 6. Charging (充電中)
- **Action**: Curled up in a floating bubble/sphere, sleeping. A battery icon fills up above.
- **Animation**: Bubble bobs gently. Zzz symbols float up. Battery bar animates filling.
- **Meaning**: 'Sleeping.' / 'Recharging.' / 'Low energy.'

### 7. Purification (淨化/退散)
- **Action**: Waves a ritual staff or hand, sweeping away 'bugs' (little black creepy crawlies).
- **Animation**: Sweeping motion. Bugs fly away or poof into smoke.
- **Meaning**: 'Fixing bugs.' / 'Deleting bad vibes.' / 'Begone!'

### 8. Full Power (全開)
- **Action**: Eyes open wide glowing, hair floating up, intense aura.
- **Animation**: Aura flares up like flames (but digital). Background speeds up.
- **Meaning**: 'Let us do this!' / 'Serious mode.' / 'High performance.'

## Production Notes
- **Aspect Ratio**: W320 x H270 (Standard LINE Animation)
- **Loop**: 1-4 seconds per sticker.
- **Key Detail**: Ensure the runes on the gloves are visible in hand gestures, as they are a key identifier.
